Mossling Privacy Policy

中文版本

Effective: November 1, 2026

The short version: Your data stays on your iPhone. Mossling has no accounts and no servers, and it doesn't send your data to us or anyone else.

What we read

With your permission, Mossling reads sleep, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, steps, and workout times from the Health app to work out what might suit your day. It only reads. It never writes anything to Health.

What you add

Your moods, tags, mood notes, habits and check-ins, manually logged sleep, your companion's name, and your settings are all things you enter yourself.

Where it's kept

All of this is stored on your iPhone. It isn't uploaded to a server, saved to iCloud, or included in iPhone backups. That means your records won't move to a new phone by themselves, but you can export a copy in Settings.

What we don't do

Mossling doesn't send your health data or anything you write. It has no ads or analytics tools. Your data isn't sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Notifications

Notifications are created on your iPhone. The morning hello may mention how you're doing today and why (like a short night's sleep), and it can appear on your Lock Screen. You can turn reminders off in Settings, or change how notifications appear in iPhone Settings.

Exporting

An export creates a JSON file, and you choose where it goes. Once it leaves Mossling, it's in the hands of whichever service or person you shared it with.

Deleting

Choosing "Delete all data" in Settings, or deleting the app, removes the records Mossling keeps. This doesn't change anything in the Health app. Health and notification permissions are managed by your iPhone, and you can change them in iPhone Settings.

Diagnostics from Apple

If you choose to share analytics with app developers on your iPhone, Apple may give us summarized usage information and crash reports. These don't include your health data or anything you write.

Medical note

Mossling is for everyday wellness. It doesn't give medical advice and can't diagnose or treat anything.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.

Contact

Leave a message on GitHub.

Mossling（苔苔）隐私政策

English

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 1 日

简单来说： 你的数据只留在你的 iPhone 上。Mossling 没有账号，没有服务器，也不会把你的数据发给我们或任何人。

我们读取什么

经过你的允许，Mossling 从“健康”App 读取睡眠、心率变异性、静息心率、步数和运动时段，用来判断今天适合做什么。Mossling 只读取，不会向“健康”App 写入任何内容。

你写下的内容

心情、标签、心情备注、习惯和打卡、手动记录的睡眠、宠物名字和设置，都由你自己输入。

数据存在哪里

以上内容都保存在你的 iPhone 上，不上传服务器，不存 iCloud，也不包含在 iPhone 的备份里。所以换手机时记录不会自己跟过去，你可以在设置里导出一份。

我们不做什么

Mossling 不发送健康数据或你写下的内容，不接入广告或分析工具，不出售或共享你的数据，也不会把它用于广告。

通知

通知由 iPhone 在本机生成。早安问候可能写到你今天的状态和原因（比如“昨晚睡得有点少”），会显示在锁屏上。你可以在设置里关掉提醒，也可以在 iPhone 设置里调整通知的显示方式。

导出

导出会生成一份 JSON 文件，由你决定存到哪里或发给谁。文件离开 Mossling 之后，就由你选择的服务或收件人来保管。

删除

在设置里选择“删掉所有数据”，或者卸载 App，都会删除 Mossling 保存的记录。这不会改动“健康”App 里的数据。健康和通知的权限由 iPhone 管理，可以在 iPhone 设置里更改。

Apple 的诊断数据

如果你在 iPhone 上选择与 App 开发者共享分析数据，Apple 可能向我们提供汇总的使用情况和崩溃报告。这些内容不包含你的健康数据或你写下的内容。

医疗说明

Mossling 用于日常健康参考，不提供医疗建议，也不能诊断或治疗任何问题。

政策更新

如果这份政策有变化，我们会更新这个页面和上面的日期。
